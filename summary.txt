Executive Summary – GitLab CI/CD Adoption Module

This Python module provides a comprehensive, enterprise-ready view of GitLab CI/CD adoption across a group or organization, working seamlessly with GitLab SaaS and self-managed instances.

What it does

Measures CI/CD adoption coverage across all projects in a group (including subgroups)

Analyzes pipelines, jobs, and stages from the last N days (default 30)

Classifies jobs using a hybrid model (stage name first, job name fallback) for high accuracy

Identifies CI/CD maturity patterns per project

Key insights delivered

% of projects using CI/CD

% of projects actively running pipelines

Average jobs per pipeline (CI complexity)

Job distribution across Build, Test, Security, Deploy

Presence of key pipeline stages

Automatic classification into maturity profiles:

Advanced CI/CD

Standard CI/CD

CI Only

CD Only

Basic / Ad-hoc

Why it matters

Works reliably even with inconsistent job or stage naming

Enables governance, DevOps maturity assessment, and platform ROI tracking

Produces data-frame and CSV outputs ready for executive dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Excel)

Who it’s for

Platform engineering & DevOps leaders

Security & compliance teams

Engineering management tracking CI/CD adoption at scale

In short, this module turns raw GitLab pipeline data into clear, actionable CI/CD adoption and maturity metrics suitable for executive reporting.
